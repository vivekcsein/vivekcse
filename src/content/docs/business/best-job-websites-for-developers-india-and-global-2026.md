# Top Job Websites for Software/Developer Roles — India & Outside India (2026)

> Written for software engineers, full-stack/backend/frontend devs, and technical job-seekers — not general job hunters. Split into an **India section** and a **Global/outside-India section**, since the platforms that actually work differ a lot between the two markets.

---

## 1. How to think about this before you start applying

Job platforms fall into a few distinct buckets, and mixing them up wastes weeks:

| Type                                                                      | What it does                                                                    | Use it when…                                                                            |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Mass job boards** (Naukri, Indeed, LinkedIn)                            | Huge volume, huge competition, resume-database model                            | You want maximum reach and are applying at scale                                        |
| **AI/algorithm-matched tech platforms** (Instahyre, Cutshort, Hired)      | You build one strong profile, companies express interest first                  | You have a solid resume/projects and want signal over volume                            |
| **Startup-specific boards** (Wellfound, Y Combinator's Work at a Startup) | Salary/equity shown upfront, direct founder contact                             | You want early-stage/startup roles, not big-corp                                        |
| **Vetted global marketplaces** (Turing, Toptal, Arc.dev)                  | Hard technical screening once, then matched to international (often US) clients | You want to work for foreign companies without relocating, and can pass a rigorous test |
| **Compensation-first platforms** (Levels.fyi Jobs, Hired)                 | Pay transparency and reverse-marketplace (companies apply to you)               | You're optimizing specifically for total comp                                           |
| **Remote-only aggregators** (RemoteOK, Himalayas, We Work Remotely)       | Filtered to remote-first companies globally                                     | You want location-independent work specifically                                         |

A useful rule that shows up across almost every 2026 comparison: **don't rely on one platform.** A layered stack — one broad board for coverage, one specialist/AI-matched platform for signal, and one niche board for your specific goal (remote, startup, comp) — consistently outperforms grinding a single site.

## 2. India — top platforms

### Naukri.com

India's largest and oldest job portal, still the default for IT-services-heavy hiring (TCS, Infosys, Wipro-type companies) and the biggest resume database in the country. Naukri's own JobSpeak Index reportedly showed hiring activity up meaningfully year-on-year into late 2025, so overall volume remains strong.

- **Best for:** Broad reach, especially outside Tier-1 cities, and IT services / MNC bulk hiring.
- **Reality check:** Very high applicant volume per posting means your resume can get buried — pair it with a specialist platform below rather than relying on it alone.
- Link: https://www.naukri.com

### Instahyre

An AI-matching platform ("InstaMatch") used by companies including Google, Amazon, and Microsoft in India. Instead of applying to postings, you build a detailed profile and express interest; the algorithm surfaces you to relevant recruiters, and companies you match with reach out first.

- **Best for:** 2–10 years experienced engineers targeting product companies and well-funded startups.
- **Watch out for:** Profile quality matters a lot — if your resume doesn't clear their baseline bar, match quality drops sharply. Not the easiest platform for freshers.
- Link: https://www.instahyre.com

### Hirist

Naukri's tech-focused sister platform, positioned specifically for backend, frontend, mobile, data, and DevOps roles, drawing from an IIT/NIT-heavy graduate pool with AI-driven screening. Reported as used by 100+ unicorns for engineering hiring.

- **Best for:** Mid-to-senior tech-specific roles where you want a more filtered pool than Naukri's general listings.
- Link: https://www.hirist.com

### Cutshort

Combines AI/ML-based matching with a startup- and product-company-heavy candidate pool, particularly strong for 0–5 years experience. Popular with companies like Razorpay, Freshworks, and Series A/B startups.

- **Best for:** Early-to-mid career engineers targeting the Indian startup ecosystem; also has community/networking features.
- Link: https://cutshort.io

### Wellfound (formerly AngelList Talent)

Global startup job board with a strong India presence — every listing typically shows funding stage, team size, and salary/equity range upfront, so you don't need three recruiter calls just to find out the band.

- **Best for:** Startup roles (seed to Series C), remote-friendly positions, and getting direct access to founders/hiring managers instead of HR gatekeeping.
- Link: https://wellfound.com

### IIMJobs (and its Hirist sister listings)

Originally built for management roles, but its tech/senior-engineering listings (via the Hirist family) are relevant once you're targeting leadership or senior IC tracks.

- **Best for:** Senior/lead engineering and engineering-management roles.
- Link: https://www.iimjobs.com

### Foundit (formerly Monster India)

Broad mid-management and general reach platform — a reasonable secondary board for coverage beyond Naukri.

- Link: https://www.foundit.in

### GeeksforGeeks Jobs

The recruitment arm of the well-known coding-education platform, tailored specifically toward developers who engage with coding challenges and DSA practice. Strong fit if your strength is algorithmic/competitive programming.

- **Best for:** Fresh graduates and competitive programmers with strong DSA fundamentals.
- Link: https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/jobs

### HackerEarth

A coding-assessment and hackathon-driven hiring platform — companies evaluate you through real coding challenges rather than resume screening alone, which favors candidates who are stronger at proving skill than at writing resumes.

- **Best for:** Skill-first hiring, hackathon participants, and candidates who'd rather demonstrate ability than sell it on paper.
- Link: https://www.hackerearth.com

### Internshala

The default platform for internships and entry-level/fresher roles. Not useful once you have a few years of experience.

- **Best for:** Students and freshers only.
- Link: https://internshala.com

## 3. Outside India — top global platforms

### LinkedIn Jobs

Technically a professional network more than a job board, but its scale (1B+ members) and context — mutual connections, hiring-manager activity, company insight — make it close to mandatory for mid-career and senior roles internationally. The real value is using it deliberately (direct outreach, referrals) rather than passively applying.

- **Best for:** Mid-to-senior roles anywhere in the world, and building the relationships that lead to referrals.
- Link: https://www.linkedin.com/jobs

### Wellfound (global)

The default board for startup hiring worldwide, not just India — funding stage, team size, and salary/equity shown per listing. Reported to have one of the better reply rates among branded job boards in independent studies.

- **Best for:** Startup-native talent wanting direct founder access instead of a corporate funnel.
- Link: https://wellfound.com

### Turing

AI-powered developer-matching platform connecting remote developers (a large share based in India and other non-US countries) with mostly US companies. Runs a multi-hour technical vetting process (coding exercises + interviews), then matches you to client roles.

- **Best for:** Indian/global developers who want to work for US companies without relocating, and can pass a rigorous, multi-stage technical screen.
- **Reality check:** Turing does not publish a public rate card for developers, and third-party analyses estimate the platform retains a significant share of the client's bill rate as margin — so treat any quoted number as a starting point for negotiation, not a fixed figure.
- Link: https://www.turing.com

### Toptal

The "top 3%" vetted network — rigorous technical screening (tests, code reviews, live interviews), after which you're matched to long-term client retainers rather than one-off gigs. Rates skew premium.

- **Best for:** Senior developers who can pass a hard bar and prefer fewer, longer engagements over constant pitching.
- Link: https://www.toptal.com

### Arc.dev

Remote-first network blending vetted matching with more traditional job listings, covering developers, designers, and technical marketers at startups and tech companies globally.

- Link: https://arc.dev

### Dice

The strongest platform specifically for **contract/IT staffing** roles in the US tech market — a good pairing with LinkedIn for recruiter inbound on contract-heavy openings.

- **Best for:** Contract and staffing-style tech roles, primarily US-facing.
- Link: https://www.dice.com

### Levels.fyi Jobs

Built around compensation transparency — median software-engineer pay data (base, equity, bonus) at companies including FAANG-tier firms is public, so you can benchmark an offer before you even apply.

- **Best for:** Setting a realistic salary floor before negotiating, especially for US-market roles. Mostly US-focused for compensation data, and limited to companies in its database.
- Link: https://www.levels.fyi/jobs

### Hired

A reverse-marketplace model — you build one strong profile with disclosed compensation ranges, and vetted companies apply to interview _you_, rather than the other way around.

- **Best for:** Experienced engineers who want inbound interest instead of cold-applying.
- Link: https://hired.com

### Y Combinator's Work at a Startup

Direct pipeline into YC-backed companies — useful specifically for early-stage startup roles with high growth/equity upside.

- **Best for:** Developers who want to join an early-stage startup and are comfortable with startup risk/pace.
- Link: https://www.workatastartup.com

### RemoteOK

Tag-based, remote-native job board with salary data and strong stack-based filtering (React, Go, Rust, etc.). Some filters/salary data have moved behind a paywall in recent updates — check current access before relying on it.

- **Best for:** Filtering remote roles precisely by tech stack.
- Link: https://remoteok.com

### Himalayas

Clean, modern remote-job board showing salary and timezone info on every listing, with some built-in AI tools for job matching/resume tailoring. Frequently cited as one of the better free, transparent options in 2026 comparisons.

- **Best for:** Remote-only roles globally, with less noise than larger generalist boards.
- Link: https://himalayas.app

### We Work Remotely

One of the longest-running remote job boards — free to browse and apply, employer-pays-to-post model, no commission on either side.

- **Best for:** Longer-term remote roles, broad tech + non-tech coverage.
- Link: https://weworkremotely.com

## 5. A practical stack (pick 3–5, not 15)

**If you're in India, targeting Indian companies:**

1. Naukri — for coverage
2. Instahyre or Hirist — for tech-specific signal
3. Wellfound — for startup roles with transparent comp
4. LinkedIn — for referrals and senior roles

**If you're in India, targeting global/remote/US roles:**

1. Turing — for a vetted path into US client work without relocating
2. Wellfound (remote filter) — for international startup roles
3. LinkedIn — direct outreach to hiring managers abroad
4. RemoteOK / Himalayas — for remote-native listings filtered by stack

**If you're outside India, targeting global/US tech roles:**

1. LinkedIn — relationship-driven, essential for mid/senior
2. Wellfound or Y Combinator's Work at a Startup — for startup-stage roles
3. Levels.fyi Jobs — to benchmark comp before negotiating
4. Dice (if contract work) or Toptal/Arc.dev (if consulting-style)

## 6. Checklist before you start applying

- [ ] Pick **one** primary volume board and **one** specialist/AI-matched board — don't spread across ten platforms with a thin profile on each.
- [ ] Fill your profile completely before relying on match-based platforms (Instahyre, Cutshort, Hired) — thin profiles get filtered out by their algorithms before a human ever sees them.
- [ ] For vetted marketplaces (Turing, Toptal), budget real prep time for the technical screen — it's a multi-hour, multi-stage process, not a form you fill out.
- [ ] If targeting remote/global roles, check salary/timezone requirements up front (Wellfound, Himalayas, Levels.fyi all show this) rather than discovering a mismatch after interviews.
- [ ] Treat job boards as the _discovery_ layer — the highest-signal outcome is still a warm referral or direct hiring-manager outreach on LinkedIn, not a cold application.
- [ ] Watch for remote-job scams: verify any opening on the employer's own official site, and never pay for the promise of a job or interview.
